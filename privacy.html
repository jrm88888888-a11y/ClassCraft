Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

ClassCraft is a free educational resource for GCSE and A-Level students. We believe in keeping things simple, so this policy is short: we collect as little data as possible and we never sell or share your information.

What we collect

ClassCraft does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use the site. You can play every game, use every flashcard, and read every book without giving us anything at all.

The only time we collect personal information is if you choose to contact us via the contact form. In that case, we receive the name, email address, and message you submit. This data is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service, and delivered to us by email.

Cookies and tracking

ClassCraft does not use cookies, analytics software, or any tracking scripts. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar service. Your browsing activity on this site is not tracked or recorded.

Third-party services

The site relies on a small number of external services:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions. Their privacy policy is available on the Formspree website.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used on this site. Google may log standard connection data (such as your IP address) when fonts are loaded.

No other third-party services receive data from this site.

Children's privacy

ClassCraft is designed for students and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The contact form is the only feature that accepts personal data, and use of it is entirely optional.

Data storage and security

ClassCraft is a static website with no database and no user accounts. Contact form submissions are stored by Formspree and delivered to us via email. We do not store form data on any server of our own.

Your rights

If you have submitted a contact form and would like your data removed, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top. Given the simplicity of the site, changes are unlikely to be frequent.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us via the contact page.
