Why I built this

MartinEdu started from a simple frustration: the best revision resources were either behind paywalls, buried in poorly designed websites, or just not very good. Students deserve better than that.

So I built what I wanted to exist — 160 interactive revision games, a vocabulary tester covering over 3,500 terms across 12 exam boards, classroom simulations, and a growing library of subject jokes to make the whole thing slightly less grim.

Everything on this site is free. No subscription, no login, no ads. Open a game and start revising.

Who I am

I’m James Martin — a Physics graduate from the University of Bristol and a qualified teacher with a PGCE from the University of Oxford. I’ve taught in secondary schools and universities across the UK and internationally, covering everything from KS3 Science to undergraduate business ethics.

I also write GCSE revision guides published on Amazon under the name James R. Martin, and I’ve spent the last several years developing teaching materials that actually work — activity-based, visually clear, and built around how memory and learning really function.

What’s on the site

The site is growing. Right now you’ll find revision games for nine subjects at GCSE and A-Level, a full vocabulary testing hub with six different game modes, interactive classroom simulations, and subject-specific jokes that are marginally better than the ones in Christmas crackers.

New resources are added regularly. If there’s a subject or topic you’d like to see covered, get in touch.
